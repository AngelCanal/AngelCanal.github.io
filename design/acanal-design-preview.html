04 / A question I work on

How can the timing of neural activity inform stimulation for epilepsy?

About me

I'm a postgraduate researcher in the Microelectronics Lab at the University of Glasgow. I use computational models to generate brain-inspired signals for neuromodulation, and study how their timing can inform stimulation for epilepsy.

My work connects computational models with in vitro experiments. I also work on bioactive, adaptive coatings for neural probes, looking at the materials between a device and living tissue.
