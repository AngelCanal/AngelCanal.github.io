04 / Beyond the work

ac.

At the interface
of brain & machine.

At the University of Glasgow's Microelectronics Lab, my research brings together AI, dynamical systems, and neural engineering. I work on generating brain-inspired signals for neuromodulation and on bioactive, adaptive coatings for neural probes.

My work connects computational models with in vitro experiments and the materials used at the interface between devices and living tissue.
