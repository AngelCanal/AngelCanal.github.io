## The question

Can stimulation restore communication across a disrupted hippocampal circuit by following its own ongoing activity?

## The study

In a brain-slice model, CA3 interictal discharges triggered stimulation at the subiculum. This created an artificial route for hippocampal output to reach the entorhinal cortex.

The paper reports reduced or absent ictal activity in the slice experiments and restored functional connectivity under the tested conditions. The central idea is to let activity from a biological circuit set the timing of an artificial connection.
