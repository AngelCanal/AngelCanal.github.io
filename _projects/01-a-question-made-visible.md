## The question

Can a small dynamical model transform cortical recordings into hippocampal-inspired timing patterns?

## The study

The study evaluated reservoir-generated signals against held-out cortical-CA3 recordings. A generated event schedule was then applied open loop to primary rat cortical cultures on multielectrode arrays.

The results provide an in vitro proof of concept with heterogeneous responses. The specific contribution of reservoir-derived timing remains unresolved, and closed-loop and epilepsy-model experiments remain future work.
