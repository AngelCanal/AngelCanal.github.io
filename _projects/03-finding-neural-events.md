## The question

How can relevant events be recognized in neural recordings with computationally inexpensive methods?

## The study

The paper presents ZdensityRODE and AMPDE, two approaches based on time-series segmentation. They were evaluated using epileptiform activity recorded from mouse hippocampus-cortex slices with microelectrode arrays.

The work focuses on detecting and classifying recorded events. It motivates further research toward closed-loop stimulation; seizure prediction remains an application to investigate.
